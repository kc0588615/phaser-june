# Critter Connect

A learning game about real animals: pick a place on the globe, then find which of five animals on the board is the mystery animal by matching gems to answer questions about it and ruling out the look-alikes.

## Language

### Rounds

**Trail**:
A run of 5 rounds from one place, with 3 hearts. It ends after the fifth round or the third lost round.
_Avoid_: level, campaign, session

**Heart**:
One of a trail's 3 lives; each lost round costs one.
_Avoid_: life, strike

**Round**:
One mystery animal to find, from the first move until it is found, escapes, or the moves run out.
_Avoid_: level, case, puzzle

**Mystery animal**:
The animal the round secretly picked from the suspects; the player must find it.
_Avoid_: answer, target

**Suspect**:
One of the 5 animals pinned on the board as photo cards (a polaroid on a red pin, plan 046); the mystery animal is one of them. Players see them as "the animals".
_Avoid_: candidate, option

**Look-alike**:
A suspect that isn't the mystery animal, picked for sharing many traits with the others (closest relatives first).

**Place**:
A continent picked on the globe; every suspect in a round lives there. With no place picked, animals come from the whole world.
_Avoid_: location, site, country

**Region**:
A part of a continent, such as Eastern Africa or Southern Asia, that range questions ask about.
_Avoid_: subregion, zone

### The board

**Move**:
A swap of two neighboring gems that makes a match. A round allows a limited number of moves; cascades are free.
_Avoid_: turn

**Clue gem**:
One of the 4 gem shapes that each carry one of the round's questions, shown by its face. Which shape carries which question changes every round; a shape or color never stands for a kind of trait.
_Avoid_: category gem, evidence gem

**Face**:
The emoji on a clue gem (and on its question in the grid) that says which question it fills: a sun for "Out in the day?", an egg for "Lays eggs?".
_Avoid_: icon, glyph

**Pebble**:
A plain grey gem that matches but asks nothing.
_Avoid_: blocker, filler

**Witness gem**:
A rare purple gem, collected by a match next to it, that gives a witness note.
_Avoid_: note gem

**Toy**:
A special gem a big match leaves on the board: a line gem (4 in a line) clears its row or column, a blast gem (an L or T) the gems around it, a color gem (5 in a line) every gem of one color. Matching a toy sets it off.
_Avoid_: power-up, booster, bomb, special

### Reasoning

**Question**:
One of a round's 4 yes/no questions about the mystery animal's traits. Matching 20 of its clue gem answers it; together the 4 tell every suspect apart.
_Avoid_: clue, order

**Answer**:
The reply to a question: yes or no. The mystery animal's answers fill in as questions complete; answers never rule anything out by themselves.
_Avoid_: clue, result

**Trait**:
A recorded fact about an animal that questions can ask about, such as size, diet or the regions it lives in.
_Avoid_: tag

**Field guide**:
A suspect's own record: its traits, its answers to the round's questions, and its signs. Opened by holding its photo.
_Avoid_: profile, card, dossier

**Sign**:
A short phrase from a suspect's field guide about something it does or has (digging claws, raids termite mounds).
_Avoid_: tag, hint

**Scout**:
Showing a suspect's own answer to a question by clearing that question's clue gem next to its tile. Until scouted, a suspect's answers are hidden ("?").
_Avoid_: reveal, inspect

**Pencil mark**:
The player's own guess written in a hidden answer (yes or no). The game never checks it during play.
_Avoid_: note, call

**Witness note**:
A hand-written, sourced detail about the mystery animal, with words that would name it blanked, plus how many of the suspects' field guides say the same. It fits 2 to 4 suspects, so it narrows but never solves.
_Avoid_: field note, clue, fun note, hint

**Rule out**:
The player's mark on a suspect they believe isn't the mystery animal. Free, and undone the same way until the suspect is released; undoing it stops its wander.
_Avoid_: cross out, eliminate

**Release**:
A ruled-out suspect leaving the board because a gem next to it was cleared, or because it wandered off the edge. Only ruled-out suspects can be released. A release by the player's own match leaves a line gem in its place.
_Avoid_: remove, clear

**Wander**:
A ruled-out suspect stepping one cell toward the edge after each move, the way it travels (fliers up, diggers and swimmers down, walkers to the nearer side); stepping off the edge releases it.
_Avoid_: drift, roam

**Escape**:
Releasing the mystery animal, by a clear or by its wander: the round is lost.

**Find**:
Releasing every look-alike, so the last suspect left is the mystery animal: the round is won.
_Avoid_: solve, catch, guess

**Out of moves**:
The moves ran out with more than one suspect left: the round is lost. The player may still name the animal, which counts only for the journal.
_Avoid_: forced guess, timeout

### Rewards and records

**Star**:
A round's rating: one for a find, more for finding with moves to spare.

**Streak**:
Finds in a row; a lost round ends it.

**Journal**:
The player's record of the animals they've found or named, kept on the device (and synced when signed in).
_Avoid_: collection, log, album

**Source**:
Where a trait's or witness note's fact comes from, such as the IUCN Red List or Animal Diversity Web. Players can open it to check and read more once the round is over.
_Avoid_: citation, reference
