# Critter Connect

A learning game about real animals: pick a place on the globe, then name the mystery animal by matching gems to earn questions about its traits.

## Language

### Rounds

**Round**:
One mystery animal to name, from the first move to the guess that ends it (right, or wrong at 0 moves).
_Avoid_: level, case

**Mystery animal**:
The animal hidden in a round that the player must name.
_Avoid_: answer, target

**Candidate**:
One of the animals shown in a round; the mystery animal is one of them.
_Avoid_: option, choice

**Look-alike**:
A candidate picked because it shares many traits with the mystery animal (after its closest relatives). A round has 12.

**Place**:
A continent picked on the globe; the mystery animal and every candidate in a round live there. With no place picked, animals come from the whole world.
_Avoid_: location, site, country

**Region**:
A part of a continent, such as Eastern Africa or Southern Asia, that Range questions ask about.
_Avoid_: subregion, zone

### Playing

**Move**:
A swap of two neighboring gems that makes a match. A round allows a limited number of moves; cascades are free.
_Avoid_: turn

**Toy**:
A special gem a big match leaves on the board: a line gem (4 in a line) clears its row or column, a blast gem (an L or T) the gems around it, a color gem (5 in a line) every gem of one color. Matching a toy sets it off.
_Avoid_: power-up, booster, bomb, special

**Category**:
One kind of trait a gem color stands for: Body, Habits, Habitat, Range or Life cycle. The rare note gem isn't a category.

**Charge**:
What a match earns in its gem's category (a match of 3 earns 1, 4 earns 2, 5 or more earns 3). It's spent on a question in that category, or, one of each color, on a family tree step.
_Avoid_: token, scan, energy, mana

**Question**:
A yes/no question the player spends a charge on to learn one of the mystery animal's traits.
_Avoid_: clue

**Trait**:
A recorded fact about an animal that questions can ask about, such as size, diet or the regions it lives in.
_Avoid_: tag

**Answer**:
The reply to a question: Yes, No or No record.
_Avoid_: clue

**No record**:
The answer to a question about a trait the field guide has no record of for the mystery animal. The charge comes back.

**Field note**:
A hand-written, sourced detail about the mystery animal, saved (sealed) for each note gem collected by a match next to it. Saved notes open only for a last chance; words that would name the animal show as blanks. It rules out nothing by itself.
_Avoid_: clue, fun note, fact, hint

**Last chance**:
One more guess after a wrong final guess, with the round's saved field notes open to read. Only if at least one note was saved.
_Avoid_: retry, extra life

**Forced guess**:
The guess a player must make when a round's moves run out.

**Streak**:
Correct guesses in a row; a wrong guess ends it.

**Source**:
Where an answer's or field note's fact comes from, such as the IUCN Red List or Animal Diversity Web. Players can open it to check and read more once the round is over (its page names the animal).
_Avoid_: citation, reference

**Family tree**:
The mystery animal's ranks from kingdom down to species. Players reveal it one step at a time (class, order, family), each step paid with one charge of every color; a step every animal left shares fills in free. The reveal card shows the whole tree.
_Avoid_: taxonomy ladder, wildcard, genus bonus
