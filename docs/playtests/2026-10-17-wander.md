## Findings

1. **Copy, fixed.** Ruling out still says "You ruled out the X. Clear a gem next to its card to release it." (`AnimalGame.tsx:368`). It doesn't say the card now walks off. Now: "It will wander off the board, or clear a gem next to its card to release it now." (wander rules only).
2. **Copy / a11y, fixed.** The announce line always says "walks": "Pig-nosed Turtle walks down", "Brown-throated Sloth walks down", "Hispaniolan Solenodon walks down" (`ClueBoardScene.ts:99`). Now uses the travel verb: flies up, swims down, digs down, walks left/right.
3. **UX.** A marked card gives no hint of its direction until it moves (plan open item "Seeing the way"). The direction matters for the call-back and for aiming.
4. **UX, fixed.** The reward toy appears silently: no announce line and no "reward" cue, so a player may not link it to their match. Now the move's line adds "The X left you a line gem" (only for a toy that went down). Seen: Greater Bilby released by the player's run at (5,5) → column toy there; Pig-nosed Turtle (swims down) → column toy.
5. **Feel.** Steps run one card at a time, so moves with walkers are slower. A `__cc.drag` to settled took about 0.76 s with no walkers, 1.2–1.7 s with one walker, and 2.8–3.5 s with three. Fine at round end, and noticeable when 3–4 cards are marked early.
6. **Theme / long walk.** The Brown-throated Sloth is tagged `swimmer`, so it heads down from the top row: 7 steps to leave. That's plausible (sloths climb down), but it's the "long walks" open item in the flesh.
7. **Resume works.** With no seed, a reload kept the walked bilby at (5,5) (dealt at (5,4)), along with marks, headings, released cards and moves. A `?seed=` replay never saves, by design.
