### Why

The board still works out two "+N charges" counts on every clear that it can never show, and keeps an event nothing hears, a field nothing reads and the only reader of two dead rule sets; deleting them finishes removing the 043 game. The E2E is the only check that plays the game, and its globe-key check fails about one CI run in four. A check that fails by itself teaches everyone to rerun until it passes, and a rerun also hides a real fault of the same kind. So the fixes have to make the checks trustworthy, never easier.

### Decision rules

- Delete only what Goal 1 names. Anything else that looks dead is a note in the plan, not an edit.

**Mechanism.**
- **Proven, in MapLibre:** an ease applies its last step and ends in the same render frame.
  - `_renderFrameCallback` (`ui/camera.ts:1635-1645`) runs `this._onEaseFrame(this._easeOptions.easing(t));`, then `else { this.stop(); }` once `t` reaches 1.
  - `stop()` calls `_afterEase`, which sets `this._moving = false;` (`:1394`) and fires `moveend`.

  So the first rAF sample after the ease's last frame reads `moving: false`, and its lng differs from the sample before it (the ease-out's last step, about 0.05–0.4° at a few fps). `classify` names that sample `spin`, and the gap is one sample interval. That is 128–147 ms, the frame time of a moving globe on CI's software GL.
- **Hypothesis C1:** this is the failure. It needs a sample taken *during* the 300 ms ease, which happens only when the page handled the key at once.
  - **Failing attempts:** `dLng` is exactly −36.8 in both. Every other run is less negative, so −36.8 is the bare 100 px pan: no spin frame ran between `beforeKey` and the key, so the key was handled at once and `watchGlobe` started inside the ease.

The gap counts from `keyAt`, which comes *before* that delay and the 300 ms ease. That's why they read 4079–5221, against the fling's 2958–3621 (Review corrections' 4089–4102 vs 3385–3405).
  - **Why the VPS never fails:** it is always the slow case, and throttling only makes it slower. That's why it can't be reproduced here.
  - **Supporting, not proof:** the two failures are the attempts whose page kept up. Their fling fps was 30/34 against 20. **Why it isn't loosening.**
- No number, term, constant or name changes.
- In a correct game the spin can't come back in the frame after a movement, since that movement's `moveend` armed ≥ 3000 ms.

- Measuring from `ease.end` instead of `keyAt` was considered and **rejected**: it shortens every gap by about 300–1200 ms, which also lets a too-late return (for example 6.8 s after the ease) pass the 7000 upper bound.
