### Why

The live database still holds two tables of old test rounds and accounts that no code has read or written since M6 and M11, write grants that served only the deleted save route, and a third of a view that every globe visit reads but nothing asks for. Removing them makes the game's role unable to change a row, whatever its code does, and makes `db/schema.sql` describe what the app uses. Because this changes the live database, each step must be reversible until the wait has shown nothing reads what goes, and players must see no change at all.
